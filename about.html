Why are we talking about AI?

Artificial intelligence is becoming a bigger part of our everyday lives. However, using AI also has an effect on the environment. Some of the main things we need to think about are energy, water and electronic waste.

What is the problem?

AI systems need computers and data centers to work. These computers use electricity and sometimes water for cooling. Old computer equipment can also become electronic waste.

A small reminder

AI is useful, but we should also think about how we use it and what effect it has on the environment.

What can we do?

We can use technology responsibly, avoid wasting resources, and recycle old electronic devices when possible.
